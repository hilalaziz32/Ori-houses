Theme matches the original site (kept intentionally).

- **Colors:** forest green `#1F4429` (primary), dark green `#12301d`, mid green `#3E6B4C`, sage accent `#8CC0A3` (used for highlighted words like "fast for cash"), cream `#EFE9DD` page background, card white, line `#E3DBCB`. `theme-color` meta = `#1F4429`.
- **Fonts:** Poppins (headings) + Inter (body), Google Fonts with preconnect and `display=swap`.
- **Logo assets** (client provided, in `content/`): `logo-horizontal.svg` (header), `logo-monochrome.svg`, `logo (1).svg` copied to `dist/assets/logo.svg`, `favicon.svg`, `og-default.svg`.
- **Voice:** plain, direct, reassuring, local. Short sentences. No jargon. No em dashes ([[no-em-dashes]]).
